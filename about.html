About

About this site

hasibai.com is a small independent site publishing free calculators for everyday financial, health and mathematical questions. There is no account to create, no app to install, and nothing to pay for.

How the tools are built

Every calculator runs entirely in your browser using plain JavaScript. Nothing you type is transmitted to a server, logged, or stored — close the tab and the numbers are gone.

Each tool states the formula it uses and prints the inputs it actually applied alongside the answer. That decision is deliberate: a single unexplained figure gives you no way to catch a unit mistake, and unit mistakes are by far the most common source of a wrong result.

Where the formulas come from

The finance tools use standard amortisation mathematics. The BMI categories follow World Health Organization adult ranges. The calorie tool uses the Mifflin-St Jeor equation published in 1990. Where a formula has known limitations, those are described on the page rather than hidden.

Reporting an error

If a calculator returns a result you believe is wrong, please say so — include the numbers you entered and what you expected, and it will be checked and corrected. Corrections take priority over everything else on the site.
